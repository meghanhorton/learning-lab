Reading Slider

How to use Reading Slider
  1. Enter a passage in Plain text mode, or choose CSV for multiple examples. In CSV, quote cells that contain commas or line breaks; each nonempty cell becomes one example.
  2. Choose the text size. Add letter combinations such as sh, ee or ea, and optional whole sight words, separated by commas.
  3. Dolch is off by default. Check the Dolch checkbox to combine the complete built-in list with your words, or leave it unchecked to use only your list. The words are not inserted into the exercise; matching words receive whole-word emphasis.
  4. Select Start reading. Drag the dot across the current row and release it at the end. Completed rows advance after a short pause; use Previous/Next in the bottom bar when needed.
  5. For CSV activities, use example controls/dots to change examples. On a keyboard, focus a reading dot and use arrows or Home/End; Page Up/Down changes rows.
  6. Use Edit to return to setup and Copy link to share the content and settings. In Create mode, Add to lesson saves the activity. Story Slider opens the story library when installed.

In CSV mode, quote text containing commas. A single-column text, sentence, or example header is skipped.

Reading emphasis

Includes 220 service words and 95 nouns. When checked, these are added to any whole sight words above. Off by default; leave unchecked to use only your own list. Words are highlighted only when they occur in your reading text.

Groups use a purple track and are read together with a shorter drag.
