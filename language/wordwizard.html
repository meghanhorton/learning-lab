    How to play / parent notes

    Look at the two pictures. Change the word for the left picture into the word for the right picture. Tap the letter to change, then tap its purple replacement. Read the new word, then choose Next spell. No timer or penalties. Hear the goal identifies pictures that could have more than one name, such as mat/rug or cup/mug. Hints select the correct tile and reveal the target spelling.

    Keyboard: Tab to a tile or button, then press Enter or Space. Speech uses your browser's voice; no microphone or recording is used. This game does not modify profiles or lesson data. The installed game uses the app's kitten and puppy artwork where applicable, with original soft-shaded vectors for other words. Preview pictures fall back to vectors if the app artwork cannot load.
